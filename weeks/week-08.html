Week 8

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Variables

Think of any number. Double it. Add 6. Halve the result. Subtract the number you first thought of. Your answer is 3. It is always 3, for every number in existence. How can anyone be sure without testing them all?

Open lesson → ⚛ Science

The pH Scale

Lemon juice and stomach acid are both acids, but you can drink one and the other would burn your skin. Soap and oven cleaner are both bases, but only one is safe on your hands. How do scientists measure how strong an acid or a base is?

Open lesson → 🏛 History & Geography

The Indus Valley

Four and a half thousand years ago a city in what is now Pakistan had straight streets, covered drains and a bathroom in almost every house. No palace has been found, no royal tomb, and nobody can read a word its people wrote. How do you study a civilisation that cannot speak?

Open lesson → ✒ Literature

Setting as Character

Move a story from a sunlit beach to a locked house in a snowstorm and you have a different story, even if every character stays the same. Is the setting only a background, or can a place act on a story the way a person does?

Open lesson → Ω Languages

French Immersion III: Daily Life

In French, Tuesday is mardi and Friday is vendredi. In English they are named after Tyr and Frigg. Both languages are naming the same two planets. What do the days of the week remember?
