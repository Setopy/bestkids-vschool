Week 5

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Bar Modeling II: Multi-Step Problems

Two friends start with the same amount of money. One spends $45 and the other spends $120. Now the first has four times as much as the second. With no algebra at all, can you find what they started with?

Open lesson → ⚛ Science

Molecular Bonds

Sodium is a metal that explodes in water. Chlorine is a poisonous green gas. Join them and you get table salt, which you sprinkle on your dinner. How can two dangerous things make something safe?

Open lesson → 🏛 History & Geography

The Agricultural Revolution

For nearly all of human history every person was a hunter or a gatherer. Then, in several far-apart places within a few thousand years, people began to plant seeds. It changed everything. Was it the best decision humans ever made, or a trap?

Open lesson → ✒ Literature

Fables & Aesop

A story of six sentences about a tortoise and a hare has been told for 2,500 years and is known on every continent. Why do the shortest stories last the longest?

Open lesson → Ω Languages

Latin & Greek Number Roots II

October has the root for eight but is the tenth month. December has the root for ten but is the twelfth. Did the Romans forget how to count?
