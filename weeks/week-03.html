Week 3

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Order of Operations

Type 8 + 4 × 3 into a cheap calculator and you may get 36. A scientific calculator gives 20. Both machines can add and multiply perfectly. So which one is wrong, and who says so?

Open lesson → ⚛ Science

Atomic Structure & the Bohr Model

If an atom were the size of a football stadium, its nucleus would be a small marble on the centre spot, and almost everything else would be empty. If atoms are nearly all empty space, why can you not walk through a wall?

Open lesson → 🏛 History & Geography

Biomes

A polar bear and a camel are both mammals, yet swap their homes and neither would last a week. What sorts the living world into such different zones?

Open lesson → ✒ Literature

Greek & Roman Myth

We call a weakness an "Achilles' heel", a long trip an "odyssey" and a huge ship "titanic". Why are stories told 2,800 years ago still hiding inside the words we use today?

Open lesson → Ω Languages

Morphology 101

You have probably never seen the word "unmicrowaveable", yet you understood it at once. How can you know the meaning of a word nobody ever taught you?
