Week 9

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Q1 Synthesis

For eight weeks you have collected tools: bases, primes, the order of operations, bar models, deduction, sequences and variables. A real problem never tells you which tool to pick up. How does a mathematician decide where to begin?

Open lesson → ⚛ Science

Experiment Design

A boy waters one plant with cola and one with water. The cola plant dies and he declares that cola kills plants. But the cola plant was also in a darker corner, in a smaller pot, and was a different kind of plant. What has he actually proved?

Open lesson → 🏛 History & Geography

Geographic Determinism

The first civilisations all arose in river valleys within a narrow band of latitude. Was that because of the land, or because of the people? How much of history is decided by a map?

Open lesson → ✒ Literature

Comparative Myth

Peoples in Mesopotamia, Greece, India and Mexico, who never met, all told of a great flood that drowned the world and of a few survivors in a boat. How can the same story appear on opposite sides of the Earth?

Open lesson → Ω Languages

Etymology Mapping

The word "chocolate" was born in Mexico, "algebra" in Baghdad, "window" on a Viking ship and "school" in ancient Athens. Every word you speak has travelled. Can you read a word's passport?
