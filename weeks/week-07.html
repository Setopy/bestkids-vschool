∑ Mathematics

Sequences

A teacher once told a class of small boys to add every number from 1 to 100, hoping for a quiet hour. Seven-year-old Carl Gauss wrote down 5,050 within moments. He did not add faster than the others. What did he see?

Separation Techniques

Sea water is a mixture of water and salt that no sieve in the world can separate. Yet a sailor stranded on a raft could make drinking water from it with a plastic sheet and the Sun. How?

Ancient Egypt

Egypt lasted for almost three thousand years. Cleopatra lived closer in time to us than to the builders of the Great Pyramid. What allowed one civilisation to endure so long?

Protagonists

A good author never writes "Mara was brave." The author shows Mara's hands shaking as she opens the door anyway. Why is the second version so much stronger, and how do writers make people out of ink?
