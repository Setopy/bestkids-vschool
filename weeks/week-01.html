Week 1

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Non-Decimal Bases

When you count 1, 2, 3 … up to 9, the next number suddenly needs two digits. Why does that happen at 9 and not at 7 or 15? Who decided?

Metrology & SI Units

In 1999 a spacecraft worth 125 million dollars was lost at Mars because two teams used different units. How can a unit destroy a spacecraft?

Cartography & Map Projections

On most classroom maps Greenland looks as big as Africa. In truth Africa is about fourteen times larger. Why would a map lie?

Active Reading

Two people read the same page. One remembers almost nothing. The other could teach it tomorrow. What did the second reader do differently?

The Indo-European Tree

The word for "mother" is madre in Spanish, Mutter in German, mātṛ in ancient Sanskrit and mat' in Russian. These peoples lived thousands of kilometres apart. Coincidence?
