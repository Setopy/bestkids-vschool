Week 2

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Divisibility & Primes

Every whole number greater than 1 can be built by multiplying prime numbers, and in only one way. What are these "atoms" of arithmetic, and how do you find them?

Open lesson → ⚛ Science

Particle Theory

Ice, water and steam are the same substance. If nothing is added or taken away, what actually changes when ice melts?

Open lesson → 🏛 History & Geography

Topography

Mexico City stands 2,240 metres above the sea, ringed by volcanoes. Why would millions of people choose to live so high, and how does the shape of the land decide where cities grow?

Open lesson → ✒ Literature

The Hero's Journey

A shepherd boy, a farm girl and a small creature who loves his armchair all leave home, face a great danger and return changed. Why do stories from every corner of the world follow the same road?

Open lesson → Ω Languages

Phonetics

English has 26 letters but about 44 sounds. The letters "ough" are said differently in though, through, cough and bough. If spelling cannot be trusted, how can we write down exactly what a word sounds like?
