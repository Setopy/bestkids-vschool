Week 6

Five lessons. One per subject. Work in order or follow your timetable.

∑ Mathematics

Cryptarithms

In the sum SEND + MORE = MONEY every letter hides a different digit, and there is exactly one solution. Nobody gives you a single number. How can pure logic find all eight digits?

Open lesson → ⚛ Science

Chemical vs Physical Change

Tear a sheet of paper in half and you still have paper. Burn it and you have ash and smoke, and no power on Earth will turn them back. What is the real difference between these two changes?

Open lesson → 🏛 History & Geography

Mesopotamia

The 60-minute hour, the wheel, the written word, the city and the written law all come from one strip of land between two rivers in what is now Iraq. Why there?

Open lesson → ✒ Literature

Oral Tradition

Before writing, a single storyteller could recite a poem of more than fifteen thousand lines from memory. Today most of us cannot remember a shopping list. How did they do it, and what did stories gain from living only in the voice?

Open lesson → Ω Languages

French Immersion I: Greetings

French is an official language in 29 countries on five continents, and about a third of English words came from it. If you already know Spanish and English, how much French do you know without ever having studied it?
