French Immersion II: Family & Numbers

The big questionIn French, ninety-nine is said "four-twenty-ten-nine". Why would a language count in twenties, and what does that have to do with the bases you studied in Week 1?

By the end of this lesson you will be able to

  1. Name family members in French with the correct article.
  2. Use mon, ma, mes and the verb avoir.
  3. Count from 0 to 100 in French.
  4. Say your age and describe your family in simple sentences.

1Every noun has a gender

In French, as in Spanish, every noun is either masculine or feminine. You must learn each noun together with its article.

Before a vowel or silent h, le and la both shorten to l': l'oncle, l'école. Compare the Spanish el, la, los, las.

French uses avoir for age and for possession.

J'ai dix ans. I am ten years old. J'ai un frère et deux sœurs. I have one brother and two sisters. Mon père s'appelle David. My father's name is David.

Say them: deux "duh", trois "trwa", cinq "sank", six "seess", sept "set", huit "weet", dix "deess", vingt "van". From 17 to 19 French simply says "ten-seven, ten-eight, ten-nine".

5The tens, and a surprise

For 21, 31, 41, 51 and 61 say et un: vingt et un. Otherwise join with a hyphen: vingt-deux, trente-cinq. After 60, keep counting on from sixty: 71 is soixante et onze, 75 is soixante-quinze. After 80, count on from four-twenties: 91 is quatre-vingt-onze, 99 is quatre-vingt-dix-neuf.

This is a fossil of base twenty. The Celtic peoples who lived in France before the Romans counted in twenties, perhaps on fingers and toes. English has a fossil too: "four score and seven" means 87. In Belgium and Switzerland French speakers say septante for 70 and nonante for 90.

Worked examples

Cover the steps. Predict each line before you read it.

Example 1

Say 78 and 94 in French.

  1. 78 is 60 + 18: soixante + dix-huit.
  2. 94 is 80 + 14: quatre-vingt + quatorze.

Answer: soixante-dix-huit; quatre-vingt-quatorze

Example 2

Translate: "J'ai deux frères. Ma sœur a sept ans. Mes grands-parents ont une maison."

  1. J'ai is "I have". deux frères is "two brothers".
  2. Ma sœur a sept ans: "my sister has seven years", that is, she is seven.
  3. ont is "they have". une maison is "a house".

Answer: "I have two brothers. My sister is seven years old. My grandparents have a house."

Hands-on: Mon Arbre Généalogique

You need: large paper, photographs or drawings, pens

  1. Draw your family tree with at least three generations.
  2. Label every person in French with the correct article: le père, la tante …
  3. Under each person write a sentence with their name and age: Ma mère s'appelle … Elle a … ans.
  4. Present your tree aloud in French to your family.
  5. Play Loto (bingo) with numbers from 60 to 99. The caller must say every number in French.

Practice

Write your full working in your notebook before opening an answer.

  1. How do you say "I am ten years old" in French, and what does it mean literally?

    Answer and why

    J'ai dix ans. "I have ten years."

    WhyFrench, like Spanish (tengo diez años), uses "to have" for age.

  2. Choose mon, ma or mes: ___ frère, ___ sœur, ___ parents.

    Answer and why

    mon frère, ma sœur, mes parents

    Whymon goes with a masculine noun, ma with a feminine noun, and mes with any plural.

  3. Write 16, 21 and 45 in French.

    Answer and why

    seize, vingt et un, quarante-cinq

    Why16 has its own word. 21 uses et un. Other numbers join the ten and the unit with a hyphen.

  4. What number is soixante-douze?

    Answer and why

    72

    Whysoixante is 60 and douze is 12, and 60 + 12 = 72.

  5. Write 80 and 95 in French.

    Answer and why

    quatre-vingts, quatre-vingt-quinze

    WhyEighty is "four twenties". Ninety-five is four twenties plus fifteen.

  6. Translate: "Mon oncle a trois fils."

    Answer and why

    "My uncle has three sons."

    Whya is the "he has" form of avoir, and fils is the same in singular and plural.

★ Scholar's challenge

Write five sentences in French introducing your family: how many brothers and sisters you have, their names, and three people's ages. Then write the year you were born as a number and work out how to say it in French.

Answer and why

Answers will vary. Example: "J'ai une sœur et un frère. Ma sœur s'appelle Ana. Elle a six ans." A year such as 2016 is deux mille seize.

WhyCheck each sentence for the right form of avoir, the right mon / ma / mes, and the correct number. mille is the thousand root you met in Week 5.

Socratic table talk

Discuss aloud. There is no answer key: defend your thinking with reasons.

  • Does counting "four-twenties" make arithmetic harder for French children? How could you find out?
  • Why might languages give a gender to things such as tables and bridges?
  • English says "I am ten", French and Spanish say "I have ten years". Does the way we phrase age change how we think about it?
