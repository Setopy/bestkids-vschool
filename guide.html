Parent guide

How to run BestKids V-School at home, in about three focused hours a day.

1The weekly rhythm

Each week has five lessons, one per discipline. A lesson is designed for 45 to 60 minutes of teaching and discussion, followed by independent practice.

2How each lesson is built

  • The big question. Read it aloud and wait. Let your child guess before you teach.
  • Core ideas. Read together. Stop at every bold word and ask your child to explain it back.
  • Worked examples. Cover the steps with a sheet of paper and reveal one line at a time.
  • Hands-on. Real objects first. The abstract idea sticks when the hands have met it.
  • Practice. Full working in a notebook. Open the answer only after a real attempt, and always read the why.
  • Scholar's challenge. Harder than grade level on purpose. Struggle here is the goal, not a failure.
  • Socratic table talk. Open questions with no answer key. Ask "what is your reason?" often.

3The scholar's notebooks

Keep one bound notebook per discipline. Date every page. Never erase a mistake: draw one line through it and write the correction beside it. At the end of each quarter the notebooks are the portfolio.

4Mastery before speed

Move on when your child can teach the idea back to you without the page. If a lesson needs two days, give it two days. The Singapore approach moves from concrete objects, to pictures, to symbols. If the symbols are shaky, go back to the objects.

5Tracking progress

Press Mark lesson complete at the end of a lesson. The curriculum map then shows a tick for that lesson. Progress is stored in the browser on that device only.
